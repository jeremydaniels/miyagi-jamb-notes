# JAMB Crk Notes: The Birth and Early Life of Jesus

## What JAMB expects

This topic is Section C, Topic 1 of the JAMB CRK syllabus: The birth and early life of Jesus. It covers two main content items:

*   **(a) John, the forerunner of Jesus** (Lk. 1:5-25; 57-66; 3:1-20; 7:18-35; Mk. 1:1-8; 6:14-29; Mt. 3:1-12: Matt.11:2-19; Jn. 1:6-8; 19-37; 3:22-36)
*   **(b) The birth and boyhood of Jesus** (Mt. 1:18-25; 2; Lk. 1:26-45; 2)

By the end of these notes, you should be able to:
1.  Compare the stories of the births of John and Jesus.
2.  Assess the importance of John as the forerunner of Jesus.
3.  Describe the boyhood of Jesus.

**Note on the syllabus wording:** In the syllabus, the reference for the birth of Jesus is printed with a slight typographical error as `Lk. 1:26-45;2 )`. This is intended to be Luke 1:26-45 and Luke chapter 2, which cover the annunciation to Mary, the birth of Jesus, and His boyhood.

## Content (b): The birth and boyhood of Jesus
*(This section also covers the narrative portions of Content (a) regarding John's birth, so they can be compared.)*

The birth narratives of John the Baptist and Jesus Christ are found mainly in the Gospels of Matthew and Luke. Luke provides the detailed accounts of the announcements to Zechariah and Mary, while Matthew provides the perspective of Joseph.

### The announcement of John's birth
During the reign of Herod the Great, king of Judea, there was a priest named **Zechariah** (of the priestly division of Abijah) and his wife **Elizabeth** (a descendant of Aaron). Both were righteous before God, but they were old and had no children because Elizabeth was barren.

While Zechariah was performing his priestly duties, burning incense in the temple of the Lord in Jerusalem, the **Angel Gabriel** appeared to him. The angel told him that his prayer had been heard: Elizabeth would bear a son, and he was to be named **John**. The angel said John would be great in the sight of the Lord, would drink no wine or strong drink, would be filled with the Holy Spirit from the womb, and would go before the Lord in the spirit and power of Elijah to prepare a people for the Lord.

Zechariah doubted the message because of their old age. Because of his unbelief, the angel told Zechariah he would be mute (unable to speak) until the child was born. When Zechariah came out of the temple, the people realized he had seen a vision.

### The announcement of Jesus' birth
Six months after Elizabeth conceived, God sent the Angel Gabriel to Nazareth, a city in Galilee, to a virgin named **Mary**. She was betrothed (engaged) to a man named **Joseph**, a descendant of King David. 

Gabriel greeted Mary, calling her highly favoured, and told her not to be afraid. He announced that she would conceive and bear a son, to be named **Jesus**. He would be called the Son of the Highest, and God would give Him the throne of His father David. When Mary asked how this could happen since she was a virgin, Gabriel explained that the Holy Spirit would come upon her, and the power of the Most High would overshadow her. Gabriel also told her that her relative Elizabeth was already in her sixth month of pregnancy, for "with God nothing shall be impossible" (Luke 1:37). Mary humbly accepted, saying, "Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it unto me according to thy word."

Mary then visited Elizabeth in the hill country of Judea. When Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the baby (John) leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth, filled with the Holy Spirit, blessed Mary. 

**The announcement to Joseph (Matthew 1):** Before they came together, Joseph discovered Mary was pregnant. Being a just man and not wanting to put her to public shame, he planned to divorce her quietly. But an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, telling him not to fear taking Mary as his wife, because what was conceived in her was of the Holy Spirit. The angel said she would bear a son to be named **Jesus** (meaning Saviour), for He would save His people from their sins. This fulfilled the prophecy of Isaiah that a virgin would conceive and bear a son called Emmanuel (God with us). Joseph woke up and obeyed the angel.

### The birth of John
When Elizabeth's full time came, she gave birth to a son. Her neighbors and relatives rejoiced. On the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child and wanted to name him Zechariah after his father. Elizabeth refused, saying his name must be John. The relatives argued that no one in their family had that name, so they made signs to Zechariah.

Zechariah asked for a writing tablet and wrote, **"His name is John."** Immediately, his mouth was opened, his tongue was loosed, and he began praising God. Fear came on the whole neighborhood, and people wondered, "What then will this child be?" Zechariah then prophesied about John's role in preparing the way for the Lord.

### The birth of Jesus
In those days, the Roman Emperor **Caesar Augustus** issued a decree that all the world should be registered (a census). This happened when **Quirinius** was governor of Syria. Everyone had to go to their own town to register.

Joseph travelled with Mary from Nazareth in Galilee to Bethlehem in Judea (the city of David) because he was of the house and lineage of David. While they were there, Mary went into labour. She gave birth to her firstborn son, wrapped Him in swaddling cloths, and laid Him in a **manger** (a feeding trough for animals) because there was no room for them in the inn.

That night, an angel of the Lord appeared to shepherds watching their flocks in the fields nearby, announcing the birth of the Saviour, Christ the Lord. A multitude of heavenly angels appeared praising God. The shepherds hurried to Bethlehem, found the baby in the manger, and spread the news of what they had seen before returning to glorify God.

## Objective i: compare the stories of the births of John and Jesus

JAMB frequently tests the similarities and differences between these two miraculous births. Both births were planned by God to bring about salvation.

| Feature | Birth of John the Baptist | Birth of Jesus Christ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Parents' condition** | Both parents were very old, and Elizabeth was barren. | Mary was a young, betrothed virgin; Joseph was her betrothed husband. |
| **Divine messenger** | Angel Gabriel. | Angel Gabriel (to Mary) and an unnamed angel in a dream (to Joseph). |
| **Place of announcement** | In the temple at Jerusalem, while burning incense. | In Nazareth of Galilee (to Mary) and in a dream (to Joseph). |
| **Parents' reaction** | Zechariah doubted and asked for a sign. | Mary asked how it would happen but humbly accepted in faith. |
| **Consequence of reaction**| Zechariah was struck mute until the child was born. | Mary broke into a song of praise (the Magnificat). |
| **Source of conception** | Natural biological conception, enabled by God's miracle. | Supernatural conception by the power of the Holy Spirit without a human father. |
| **The Naming** | Named "John" by the angel before conception; confirmed by Zechariah on a tablet. | Named "Jesus" by the angel before conception. |
| **Place of birth** | The hill country of Judea. | Bethlehem of Judea. |
| **First visitors** | Relatives and neighbors who came to rejoice. | Shepherds who were directed by angels. |

## Objective iii: describe the boyhood of Jesus

The boyhood of Jesus covers the events from His birth up to age twelve. These events are recorded in Matthew 2 and Luke 2.

### The Presentation in the Temple (Luke 2)
When Jesus was eight days old, He was circumcised and officially named Jesus. After the days of Mary's purification according to the Law of Moses (forty days), Joseph and Mary brought Jesus to the temple in Jerusalem to present Him to the Lord. They offered a sacrifice of a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons, which was the offering allowed for the poor.

In the temple, two prophetic people recognized the child:
1.  **Simeon:** A righteous and devout man to whom the Holy Spirit had revealed that he would not die before seeing the Lord's Christ. He took baby Jesus in his arms, blessed God, and prayed the *Nunc Dimittis* ("Lord, now lettest thou thy servant depart in peace..."). He prophesied to Mary that Jesus was appointed for the fall and rising of many in Israel, and that a sword would pierce Mary's own soul.
2.  **Anna:** A very old prophetess, a widow of eighty-four years from the tribe of Asher, who never left the temple but worshipped with fasting and prayer night and day. She gave thanks to God and spoke about the child to all who were looking for the redemption of Jerusalem.

### The Visit of the Wise Men (Matthew 2)
Sometime after Jesus' birth, Wise Men (Magi) from the East arrived in Jerusalem, asking, "Where is he who has been born king of the Jews? For we have seen his star in the East, and have come to worship him." 

King Herod the Great was deeply troubled. He gathered the chief priests and scribes, who told him that the Christ was prophesied to be born in **Bethlehem of Judea** (quoting the prophet Micah). Herod secretly called the Wise Men, found out exactly when the star appeared, and sent them to Bethlehem, lying that he also wanted to worship the child.

The star guided the Wise Men to the *house* where the child was. They rejoiced, fell down and worshipped Jesus, and opened their treasures, presenting Him with three gifts:
*   **Gold** (symbolizing kingship)
*   **Frankincense** (symbolizing priesthood and deity)
*   **Myrrh** (symbolizing suffering and death)

Being warned by God in a dream not to return to Herod, the Wise Men went back to their own country by another route.

### The Flight to Egypt and Return (Matthew 2)
After the Wise Men left, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream, warning him to take the young child and His mother and flee to **Egypt**. Herod was planning to search for the child to destroy Him. Joseph obeyed immediately, leaving by night. They stayed in Egypt until Herod's death, fulfilling the prophecy: "Out of Egypt have I called my son."

When Herod realized the Wise Men had tricked him, he became furious. He ordered the massacre of all male children in Bethlehem and the surrounding region who were **two years old and under**, according to the time he had learned from the Wise Men. 

After Herod died, an angel appeared again to Joseph in Egypt, telling him to return to the land of Israel. However, hearing that **Archelaus** (Herod's son) was reigning in Judea, Joseph was afraid to go there. Warned in another dream, he withdrew to the district of Galilee and settled in a town called **Nazareth**. This fulfilled what was spoken by the prophets, that Jesus would be called a Nazarene.

### The Boy Jesus at the Temple (Luke 2)
Jesus grew and became strong, filled with wisdom, and the favor of God was upon Him. 

Every year, His parents went to Jerusalem for the Feast of the Passover. When Jesus was **twelve years old**, they went up to the feast as usual. When the feast ended, as they returned home, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem. His parents did not know it; assuming He was among their group of travellers, they went a day's journey before they started looking for Him among their relatives. 

When they could not find Him, they returned to Jerusalem. After **three days**, they found Him in the temple, sitting among the teachers (the rabbis), listening to them and asking them questions. All who heard Him were amazed at His understanding and His answers. 

When Mary gently scolded Him, asking why He had treated them this way and saying that she and His father had been searching for Him in distress, Jesus replied:
*"How is it that ye sought me? wist ye not that I must be about my Father's business?"* (Luke 2:49). 

They did not understand this statement. Jesus went down with them to Nazareth and was obedient to them. Mary kept all these things in her heart. Jesus continued to increase in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and men.

## Content (a): John, the forerunner of Jesus

A "forerunner" is someone who goes ahead of a king or important person to announce their arrival and prepare the way for them. John the Baptist's entire life and ministry were dedicated to preparing the Jewish people for the coming of the Messiah, Jesus Christ. 

### The Preaching of John the Baptist
In the fifteenth year of the reign of Emperor Tiberius Caesar (when Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea), the word of God came to John in the wilderness. 

John lived a rugged life in the wilderness of Judea. He wore clothing made of **camel's hair** with a leather belt around his waist, and his food was **locusts and wild honey** (resembling the Old Testament prophet Elijah).

He went into all the region around the Jordan River, proclaiming a **baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins**. His core message was: 
*"Repent ye: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand."* (Matthew 3:2).

The Gospel writers state that John was the fulfillment of the prophecy in Isaiah 40:3: *"The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight."*

People from Jerusalem, all Judea, and the region around the Jordan went out to him. They confessed their sins and were baptized by him in the Jordan River. When John saw many of the religious leaders (Pharisees and Sadducees) coming for baptism, he rebuked them fiercely, calling them a "brood of vipers" and warning them that they must produce fruits in keeping with repentance, rather than just relying on being descendants of Abraham.

### John's Advice to Specific Groups (Luke 3)
The crowds asked John, "What then shall we do?" John gave practical ethical instructions:
1.  **To the multitudes:** "He who has two tunics, let him share with him who has none; and he who has food, let him do likewise." (Teachings on charity and sharing).
2.  **To the tax collectors:** "Collect no more than is appointed you." (Teachings against extortion and corruption).
3.  **To the soldiers:** "Do violence to no man, neither accuse any falsely; and be content with your wages." (Teachings against bullying, false accusation, and greed).

### John's Testimony About Jesus (John 1 and 3)
The Jewish leaders in Jerusalem sent priests and Levites to ask John who he was. John openly confessed, **"I am not the Christ."** He also denied being Elijah (in a literal, reincarnated sense) or "the Prophet". He said he was just a voice crying in the wilderness.

John pointed his own followers away from himself and toward Jesus. His key testimonies about Jesus included:
*   *"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."* (John 1:29).
*   He declared that someone was coming after him who was far greater: *"the thong of whose sandals I am not worthy to untie."*
*   He testified that while he baptized with water, the one coming would **baptize with the Holy Spirit and with fire**.
*   He testified that he saw the Holy Spirit descend from heaven like a dove and remain upon Jesus, revealing Him as the Son of God.
*   Later, when John's disciples were concerned that Jesus was gaining more followers, John expressed his joy and summed up his role as the forerunner: **"He must increase, but I must decrease."** (John 3:30).

### Jesus' Testimony About John (Matthew 11 / Luke 7)
Eventually, John was imprisoned. While in prison, he heard about the works of Christ and sent two of his disciples to ask Jesus: *"Art thou he that should come, or do we look for another?"*

Jesus did not answer with a simple "yes." Instead, He told John's disciples to go back and report what they saw and heard: the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised, and the poor have good news preached to them. These miracles were the prophesied signs of the Messiah.

After John's disciples left, Jesus spoke to the crowds about John, praising him highly:
*   He asked the crowd what they went into the wilderness to see—not a reed shaken by the wind, nor a man dressed in soft clothing (like those in kings' courts).
*   He called John **"more than a prophet"** because John was the messenger sent ahead to prepare the way.
*   Jesus declared: *"Among those born of women there has not risen anyone greater than John the Baptist; yet he who is least in the kingdom of heaven is greater than he."*
*   Jesus compared the fickle generation to children playing in the market who complained: "We piped to you, and you did not dance; we wailed, and you did not mourn." They rejected John because he fasted and drank no wine (calling him demon-possessed) and rejected Jesus because He ate and drank with sinners (calling Him a glutton and a drunkard).

### The Death of John the Baptist (Mark 6:14-29)
John the Baptist was a fearless preacher of righteousness. He boldly rebuked King **Herod Antipas** (the son of Herod the Great) because Herod had divorced his own wife to marry **Herodias**, the wife of his brother Philip. John told Herod, "It is not lawful for you to have your brother's wife." 

Herodias held a deep grudge against John and wanted him killed, but Herod Antipas feared John, knowing he was a righteous and holy man, and kept him safe in prison, even though he was perplexed when listening to him.

An opportunity arose on Herod's birthday, when he gave a banquet for his nobles, military commanders, and the leading men of Galilee. The daughter of Herodias came in and danced, pleasing Herod and his guests so much that the king promised with an oath to give her whatever she asked, up to half his kingdom.

She went to her mother for advice, and Herodias told her to ask for the head of John the Baptist. The girl immediately rushed back to the king and asked for **John the Baptist's head on a platter**. 

Herod was exceedingly sorry, but because of his oaths and his dinner guests, he did not want to refuse her. He sent an executioner to the prison, who beheaded John, brought the head on a platter, and gave it to the girl, who gave it to her mother. When John's disciples heard of this, they came, took his body, and laid it in a tomb.

## Objective ii: assess the importance of John as the forerunner of Jesus

John the Baptist played a crucial role in God's plan. His importance as the forerunner can be summarized in several points:

1.  **Fulfillment of Prophecy:** John was the fulfillment of Old Testament prophecies (Isaiah and Malachi) regarding the messenger who would come before the Lord to prepare the way.
2.  **Bridge between Testaments:** John is seen as the last and greatest of the Old Testament prophets, bridging the gap between the Old Covenant and the coming of the New Covenant in Jesus Christ.
3.  **Spiritual Preparation:** By preaching repentance and administering baptism, John prepared the hearts of the Jewish people so they would be spiritually ready to receive the Messiah.
4.  **Identification of the Messiah:** John's physical act of baptizing Jesus (covered in detail in the next topic) and witnessing the descent of the Holy Spirit allowed him to publicly identify Jesus to Israel as the "Lamb of God."
5.  **Role Model of Humility:** John perfectly understood his role. He never tried to steal the spotlight from Jesus, famously declaring, "He must increase, but I must decrease." He directed his own followers to become followers of Christ.
6.  **Champion of Social Justice:** John courageously spoke truth to power. He taught practical charity and fairness to everyday people and was martyred because he boldly condemned the immorality of the king.

## Exam tips and common mistakes

*   **Do not confuse the two Herods:** **Herod the Great** interacted with the Wise Men and ordered the massacre of the infants in Bethlehem. **Herod Antipas** (his son) is the one who imprisoned and executed John the Baptist.
*   **Remember the places:** Jesus was born in **Bethlehem**, His family fled to **Egypt**, and He grew up in **Nazareth**. 
*   **The Wise Men vs. Shepherds:** The shepherds visited Jesus at the manger on the night of His birth (Luke 2). The Wise Men visited Jesus in a *house* much later, possibly up to two years later (Matthew 2). JAMB may test this distinction.
*   **Understand the angel appearances:** Gabriel appeared physically to Zechariah and Mary while they were awake. The angel who spoke to Joseph (to take Mary as a wife, to flee to Egypt, to return) always did so *in a dream*.
*   **John's baptism:** Always remember John's baptism was a "baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins."
*   **Note who named John:** The angel Gabriel gave the name first. Elizabeth insisted on it. Zechariah confirmed it in writing. 

## Quick summary

1.  The Angel Gabriel announced the birth of John to Zechariah in the temple, and the birth of Jesus to Mary in Nazareth. Zechariah doubted and became mute; Mary believed and praised God.
2.  John was born to old, barren parents in Judea. Jesus was born to a virgin by the Holy Spirit in Bethlehem, fulfilling Old Testament prophecy.
3.  Jesus was recognized as the Messiah in the temple as a baby by Simeon and Anna, and was visited by Wise Men who brought gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
4.  To escape King Herod's slaughter of infants, Joseph and Mary fled to Egypt with Jesus, later returning to settle in Nazareth in Galilee. 
5.  At age twelve, Jesus stayed behind at the temple in Jerusalem, impressing the teachers and stating He had to be about His Father's business.
6.  John the Baptist lived in the wilderness and preached a baptism of repentance, advising the crowds, tax collectors, and soldiers to live righteous and fair lives.
7.  John's main purpose was to prepare the way for Jesus. He recognized Jesus as the "Lamb of God" and gladly accepted that his own ministry had to decrease as Jesus' ministry increased.
8.  John was beheaded by Herod Antipas because John had boldly condemned Herod's unlawful marriage to Herodias. 

## Practice questions

**1. The angel Gabriel appeared to Zechariah while he was in the temple**
- A. offering a burnt sacrifice
- B. burning incense at the altar
- C. praying with the congregation
- D. reading from the scrolls

**Answer: B.** Zechariah was chosen by lot to enter the temple of the Lord and burn incense when Gabriel appeared to him (Luke 1:9-11).

**2. Why was Zechariah struck dumb by the angel?**
- A. He laughed at the angel's appearance
- B. He asked for a sign of his wife's pregnancy
- C. He did not believe the angel's words
- D. He tried to run away from the altar

**Answer: C.** Gabriel told him he would be mute "because thou believest not my words, which shall be fulfilled in their season" (Luke 1:20).

**3. The gifts brought by the Wise Men to baby Jesus were**
- A. gold, silver, and myrrh
- B. gold, frankincense, and myrrh
- C. frankincense, myrrh, and bronze
- D. myrrh, incense, and silver

**Answer: B.** They opened their treasures and presented to Him gold, frankincense, and myrrh (Matthew 2:11).

**4. Joseph fled to Egypt with baby Jesus and Mary because**
- A. there was a severe famine in Bethlehem
- B. Augustus Caesar ordered a new census in Egypt
- C. Herod the Great wanted to destroy the child
- D. Archelaus had taken over the throne in Judea

**Answer: C.** An angel warned Joseph in a dream that Herod would search for the young child to destroy Him, and commanded them to flee to Egypt (Matthew 2:13).

**5. When Jesus was twelve years old, His parents found Him in the temple**
- A. reading the scroll of Isaiah
- B. driving out the money changers
- C. sitting among the teachers, listening and asking questions
- D. preaching repentance to the multitudes

**Answer: C.** After three days, they found Him in the temple courts, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking questions (Luke 2:46).

**6. Which prophet did John the Baptist fulfill when he cried out in the wilderness to "prepare the way of the Lord"?**
- A. Jeremiah
- B. Malachi
- C. Isaiah
- D. Ezekiel

**Answer: C.** The Gospel writers quote Isaiah: "The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord..." (Isaiah 40:3, Matthew 3:3).

**7. John the Baptist told the tax collectors who came for baptism to**
- A. stop collecting taxes for the Romans
- B. collect no more than what was appointed to them
- C. give half their wages to the poor
- D. share their tunics with those who had none

**Answer: B.** When the tax collectors asked what they should do, John replied, "Exact no more than that which is appointed you" (Luke 3:13).

**8. When John saw Jesus coming, he declared, "Behold the Lamb of God..."**
- A. who prepares the way for the Lord
- B. who baptizes with the Holy Spirit
- C. who takes away the sin of the world
- D. whose sandals I am not worthy to untie

**Answer: C.** John testified, "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world" (John 1:29).

**9. Jesus said that among those born of women, there is no one greater than**
- A. Elijah
- B. Abraham
- C. David
- D. John the Baptist

**Answer: D.** Jesus praised John highly, saying that among those born of women, there has not risen anyone greater than John the Baptist (Matthew 11:11).

**10. King Herod Antipas had John the Baptist beheaded because**
- A. John claimed to be the King of the Jews
- B. John refused to pay taxes to Rome
- C. John had rebuked Herod for marrying his brother's wife, Herodias
- D. John's disciples were causing a riot in Galilee

**Answer: C.** John was imprisoned and eventually killed because he boldly told Herod that it was not lawful for him to have his brother Philip's wife, Herodias (Mark 6:17-18).
