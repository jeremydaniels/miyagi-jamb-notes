# JAMB Crk Notes: The Second Coming of Christ

## What JAMB expects

This topic is Section D, Topic 11 of the JAMB CRK syllabus: The Second Coming of Christ. It covers the biblical teachings on the return of Jesus Christ, often called the *Parousia*. 

It has two main content areas:
- **a) The signs of the Coming of Christ** (1 Thess. 4:13-18; II Thess. 2:1-12)
- **b) Preparation for His coming** (Matt. 25:31-46; I Thess. 5:1-11; II Pet. 3:1-13)

## The signs of the Coming of Christ

**Objective i: identify the signs of the Second Coming of Christ.**
**Content a: The signs of the Coming of Christ (1 Thess. 4:13-18; II Thess. 2:1-12)**

Christians believe that Jesus Christ, who ascended into heaven after His resurrection, will return to earth in glory. This event is known as the Second Coming or the *Parousia* (a Greek word meaning "arrival" or "presence"). The early Christians eagerly expected Jesus to return in their lifetime. When time passed and some believers died, questions arose. The Apostle Paul wrote to the church in Thessalonica to clear up their confusion and explain the signs that would precede and accompany the Lord's return.

### The Great Rebellion and the Man of Lawlessness (2 Thessalonians 2:1-12)

In his second letter to the Thessalonians, Paul addressed a rumor that was causing panic. Some people were teaching that the "day of the Lord" had already come. Paul wrote to assure them that this was false. He gave them specific signs that must happen *before* Jesus returns:

1. **The Rebellion (Apostasy):** Paul stated that the day of the Lord will not come unless the "rebellion" comes first. This means there will be a great falling away from the true faith, where many people will reject God and turn to wickedness.
2. **The Man of Lawlessness Revealed:** The second major sign is the appearance of the "man of lawlessness" (also called the "son of perdition" or the "man of sin" in some translations). 
   - **His character:** He will oppose God and exalt himself over every so-called god or object of worship.
   - **His actions:** He will take his seat in the temple of God, boldly proclaiming himself to be God.
3. **The Restrainer:** Paul reminded the Thessalonians that there is currently something (or someone) restraining this lawless one, keeping him back so that he will only be revealed at his proper time. The mystery of lawlessness is already at work, but the man of lawlessness will only step forward when the restrainer is removed out of the way.
4. **Satanic Power and Deception:** When the lawless one does appear, his coming will be by the activity of Satan. He will use all kinds of power, false signs, and lying wonders to deceive people.
5. **The Delusion of the Wicked:** Because people refused to love the truth and be saved, God will send them a "strong delusion." This will cause them to believe what is false, so that all who take pleasure in unrighteousness may be condemned.

### The Immediate Signs in the Sky (1 Thessalonians 4:13-16)

While 2 Thessalonians describes historical signs leading up to the day, 1 Thessalonians 4 describes the immediate, physical signs that will announce the very moment Jesus arrives:

- **The Descent:** The Lord Himself will descend from heaven.
- **The Cry of Command:** There will be a loud, divine shout or cry of command.
- **The Voice of an Archangel:** The loud voice of a leading angel will be heard.
- **The Trumpet of God:** A great trumpet will sound to announce the arrival of the King and gather His people.

## What will happen during His Second Coming

**Objective iii: indicate what will happen during His Second Coming.**

The syllabus requires you to synthesize what the New Testament says will actually happen on the day Jesus returns. The events are described across Paul's letters, Peter's letter, and the Gospel of Matthew.

### 1. The Resurrection of Dead Believers (1 Thessalonians 4:13-18)

The Christians in Thessalonica were grieving because some of their church members had died. They feared these dead believers would miss out on the glory of Christ's return. Paul wrote to comfort them, explaining exactly what will happen to the dead in Christ:

- **Jesus brings the faithful:** Since Jesus died and rose again, God will bring with Him those who have fallen asleep (died) in Jesus.
- **The dead rise first:** When the Lord descends with the cry of command and the trumpet, the living will not have any advantage over the dead. In fact, "the dead in Christ will rise first." 
- **The Rapture (caught up):** After the dead are resurrected, those believers who are still alive on earth will be "caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air."
- **Eternal presence:** From that moment on, all believers (both the resurrected dead and the transformed living) will always be with the Lord.

### 2. The Destruction of the Lawless One (2 Thessalonians 2:8)

What happens to the man of lawlessness when Jesus returns? Paul says his end will be swift and absolute:
- The Lord Jesus will slay him with the "breath of his mouth."
- Jesus will destroy him completely by the brilliant appearance of His coming (His glory). 

### 3. Cosmic Destruction and Renewal (2 Peter 3:10)

The Apostle Peter explains that the Second Coming will not just be a political or human event; it will be a terrifying cosmic event that destroys the current physical universe to cleanse it of sin.
- The day of the Lord will come suddenly, like a thief.
- The **heavens will pass away** with a loud noise (a great roar).
- The **elements will be dissolved** with fire (melted with fervent heat).
- The **earth and all the works** that are upon it will be exposed and burned up.

### 4. The Final Judgement (Matthew 25:31-46)

Jesus Himself taught what will happen when He returns to judge the world. This is famously known as the Parable of the Sheep and the Goats.

- **The King on His Throne:** The Son of Man (Jesus) will come in His glory, accompanied by all the angels, and He will sit on His glorious throne.
- **The Gathering:** All the nations of the earth will be gathered before Him.
- **The Separation:** He will separate people one from another, just as a shepherd separates sheep from goats. 
- **The Sheep on the Right:** He will place the sheep (the righteous) on His right hand.
- **The Goats on the Left:** He will place the goats (the wicked) on His left hand.
- **The Verdict:** He will invite the righteous into eternal life and the Kingdom, and He will banish the wicked into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels.

| --- | --- | --- |
| **The descent** | 1 Thessalonians 4 | Jesus descends with a shout, archangel's voice, and God's trumpet. |
| **The resurrection** | 1 Thessalonians 4 | The dead in Christ rise first, then living believers are caught up into the clouds. |
| **Defeat of Evil** | 2 Thessalonians 2 | Jesus destroys the man of lawlessness with the breath of His mouth. |
| **Cosmic burning** | 2 Peter 3 | The heavens pass away, elements melt, and the earth is burned up. |
| **The Final Judgement** | Matthew 25 | All nations are gathered; the righteous (sheep) and wicked (goats) are separated for eternal reward or punishment. |

## Preparation for His coming

**Objective ii: specify the preparations for His coming.**
**Content b: Preparation for His coming (Matt. 25:31-46; I Thess. 5:1-11; II Pet. 3:1-13)**

Because the exact time of the Second Coming is unknown, the Bible repeatedly emphasizes that believers must be prepared at all times. The syllabus passages offer three distinct ways to prepare: spiritual vigilance, holy living, and active charity.

### 1. Spiritual Vigilance and Sobriety (1 Thessalonians 5:1-11)

Paul told the Thessalonians that there was no need to write to them about specific dates and times, because they already knew that **"the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night."** 

- **Sudden destruction:** While people in the world are saying, "There is peace and security," sudden destruction will fall upon them as suddenly as labor pains come upon a pregnant woman, and they will not escape.
- **Children of Light:** Believers, however, are not in darkness. Therefore, that day should not surprise them like a thief. They are "children of light and children of the day."
- **How to prepare:**
  1. **Stay awake and be sober:** Do not sleep spiritually (living in sin or ignorance) as others do. Those who get drunk are drunk at night, but believers belong to the day and must be sober-minded.
  2. **Put on spiritual armor:** To be prepared, Christians must put on the **breastplate of faith and love**, and for a **helmet the hope of salvation**.
  3. **Encourage one another:** Because God has destined believers not for wrath but to obtain salvation through Jesus, they must build each other up and comfort one another.

### 2. Holy Living in the Face of Delay (2 Peter 3:1-13)

In Peter's time, people were beginning to doubt that Jesus would ever return. False teachers were mocking the church.

- **The Mockers (Scoffers):** Peter warned that in the last days, scoffers will come, following their own sinful desires. They will ask, *"Where is the promise of his coming? For ever since the fathers fell asleep, all things have continued as they were from the beginning of creation."*
- **The Reality of God's Judgement:** Peter reminds them that they deliberately forget history. God once destroyed the ancient world with water (Noah's flood). By that same word, the present heavens and earth are being stored up for fire, kept for the day of judgement and the destruction of the ungodly.
- **The Reason for the Delay:** Why hasn't Jesus returned yet? Peter explains that God's view of time is different from ours: **"With the Lord one day is as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day."** God is not slow about His promise. Instead, He is being patient, not wanting anyone to perish, but wanting everyone to reach repentance.
- **How to prepare:**
  1. Since the world will be destroyed by fire, believers must live **lives of holiness and godliness**.
  2. They should **wait for and hasten** the coming of the day of God.
  3. They should look forward to the **new heavens and a new earth** in which righteousness dwells.

### 3. Active Charity: The Sheep and the Goats (Matthew 25:31-46)

Jesus taught that true preparation is not just looking at the sky, but actively loving other people. In the Parable of the Sheep and the Goats, the standard for judgement at the Second Coming is how people treated the vulnerable and needy in society.

When the King separates the nations, He will say to the **sheep on His right**: *"Come, O blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world."* 
Why? Because they practiced active charity. Jesus gives six specific acts of mercy:
1. "I was hungry and you gave me food."
2. "I was thirsty and you gave me drink."
3. "I was a stranger and you welcomed me."
4. "I was naked and you clothed me."
5. "I was sick and you visited me."
6. "I was in prison and you came to me."

The righteous will be surprised and ask, "Lord, when did we see you hungry or thirsty...?" The King will answer: **"Truly, I say to you, as you did it to one of the least of these my brethren, you did it to me."**

Then He will turn to the **goats on His left** and say, *"Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels."* They are condemned because they failed to do these acts of charity. When they ask when they saw Him in need, He will reply: **"As you did it not to one of the least of these, you did it not to me."**

**Practical application in Nigeria:** To prepare for the Second Coming, Christians are called to care for the "least of these" in their communities. This means providing food for the poor, visiting patients in local hospitals, assisting internally displaced persons (IDPs), welcoming strangers, and showing practical love rather than just attending church services.

| **Character** | Righteous | Wicked / Cursed |
| **Action** | Showed mercy and practical charity to the needy | Ignored the needs of the vulnerable |

| **Destiny** | Inherit the kingdom / Eternal life | Eternal fire (prepared for the devil) / Eternal punishment |

## The importance of His coming

**Objective iv: examine the importance of His coming.**

The doctrine of the Second Coming is central to Christian theology. It is important for several reasons:

1. **Vindication of God's Justice:** The present world is full of injustice, corruption, and the suffering of the innocent. The Second Coming brings the Final Judgement, where God will right all wrongs, punish the wicked, and reward the righteous.
2. **Defeat of Evil:** The return of Christ will bring about the final destruction of the man of lawlessness, Satan, and all forces of evil, fulfilling God's promise to conquer sin completely.
3. **Resurrection and Eternal Life:** It guarantees that death is not the end. The dead in Christ will be resurrected to eternal life, giving believers hope that they will be reunited with their loved ones who have died in the faith.
4. **Motivation for Holy Living:** Knowing that Christ will return suddenly "like a thief" encourages Christians to stay spiritually awake, avoid sin, and live holy, sober lives of faith and love.
5. **Encouragement in Suffering:** The promise of a new heaven and a new earth provides immense comfort to Christians undergoing persecution, sickness, or hardship, reminding them that their present suffering is temporary.
6. **Focus on Practical Love:** As seen in Matthew 25, the reality of the coming judgement forces believers to focus on practical charity—caring for the poor, the sick, and the marginalized—because faith without works will not stand on the last day.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Parousia | A Greek word meaning "presence" or "arrival," used to describe the Second Coming of Christ. |
| Apostasy (The Rebellion) | A great falling away from the true faith that will precede the Second Coming. |
| Man of Lawlessness | An evil figure (often identified as the Antichrist) who will exalt himself as God and deceive many before Christ destroys him. |
| Rapture | The event described in 1 Thessalonians 4 where living believers are "caught up" in the clouds to meet the descending Lord in the air. |
| The Day of the Lord | The biblical term for the time when God will intervene in human history to judge the wicked and save the righteous. |
| Scoffers / Mockers | People described by Peter who will laugh at the idea of Christ's return, claiming the world has always remained exactly as it is. |

## Exam tips and common mistakes

- **Distinguish between the Thessalonian letters:** 1 Thessalonians 4 is about the **dead in Christ rising** and the rapture. 1 Thessalonians 5 is about the **thief in the night** and spiritual vigilance. 2 Thessalonians 2 is about the **rebellion and the man of lawlessness**.
- **The "Thief in the Night":** This metaphor specifically refers to the *suddenness* and *unexpectedness* of Christ's return, not to Jesus acting like a criminal.
- **Why the delay?** According to 2 Peter 3, the delay in Christ's return is due to **God's patience**, because He wants everyone to repent, not because God has forgotten His promise.
- **The basis of judgement:** In Matthew 25, the sheep and goats are separated based purely on **how they treated the needy and vulnerable** (feeding the hungry, clothing the naked, visiting the sick/prisoners). 
- **The sequence in 1 Thess 4:** Remember the order: Jesus descends with a shout/trumpet -> The dead in Christ rise *first* -> Then the living are caught up with them.
- **Cosmic destruction:** It is 2 Peter 3 that explicitly mentions the heavens passing away with a loud noise and the elements melting with fire. 

## Quick summary

1. The Second Coming of Christ (Parousia) will be preceded by the Great Rebellion (apostasy) and the revelation of the man of lawlessness, who will deceive people with false signs.
2. Christ's arrival will be announced by a loud command, the voice of an archangel, and the trumpet of God.
3. The dead in Christ will be resurrected first, and then living believers will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord.
4. The Second Coming will bring cosmic destruction; the heavens will pass away and the elements will dissolve with fire, making way for a new heaven and a new earth.
5. The day of the Lord will come suddenly, like a thief in the night, so believers must prepare by staying awake, being sober, and living holy lives.
6. The delay of the Second Coming is a sign of God's patience, giving humanity time to repent.
7. At the Final Judgement, Jesus will separate the righteous (sheep) from the wicked (goats) based on their practical charity and care for the needy.
8. The Second Coming is important because it brings ultimate justice, defeats evil, resurrects believers, and establishes God's eternal kingdom.

## Practice questions

**1. According to Paul in 2 Thessalonians, which of the following must happen before the day of the Lord comes?**

- A. The dead in Christ must rise first
- B. The elements must melt with fervent heat
- C. The rebellion and the revelation of the man of lawlessness
- D. The separation of the sheep and the goats

**Answer: C.** Paul states in 2 Thessalonians 2:3 that the day will not come unless the rebellion comes first and the man of lawlessness is revealed.

**2. In 1 Thessalonians 4, what did Paul teach would happen to the dead in Christ when Jesus returns?**

- A. They will face the Final Judgement first
- B. They will rise first before the living are caught up
- C. They will remain asleep until the new earth is created
- D. They will be judged alongside the man of lawlessness

**Answer: B.** Paul comforts the church by explaining that the dead in Christ will rise first, and then those who are alive will be caught up together with them.

**3. According to 1 Thessalonians 5, the day of the Lord will come suddenly like a**

- A. roaring lion
- B. flash of lightning
- C. thief in the night
- D. flood in the days of Noah

**Answer: C.** Paul uses the metaphor of a "thief in the night" to describe the sudden and unexpected nature of the Second Coming.

**4. Based on Matthew 25, the King will invite the sheep on His right to inherit the kingdom because they**

- A. preached the gospel to all nations
- B. prayed and fasted continuously
- C. offered the correct sacrifices in the temple
- D. fed the hungry, clothed the naked, and visited the sick

**Answer: D.** The righteous are rewarded for their practical charity to the needy, which Jesus counts as being done to Him.

**5. According to Peter, why is God delaying the Second Coming of Christ?**

- A. Because the man of lawlessness is not yet ready
- B. Because God is patient and wants all to reach repentance
- C. Because the angels have not finished preparing the trumpets
- D. Because the restrainer is still holding Satan back

**Answer: B.** 2 Peter 3:9 states that the Lord is not slow, but is patient, not wanting anyone to perish but everyone to come to repentance.

**6. How will the Lord Jesus destroy the man of lawlessness when He returns, according to 2 Thessalonians?**

- A. With the breath of His mouth and His glorious appearance
- B. By casting him into a lake of fire alongside the goats
- C. By commanding an archangel to bind him for a thousand years
- D. With the trumpet of God that dissolves the elements

**Answer: A.** 2 Thessalonians 2:8 says the Lord Jesus will kill the lawless one with the breath of His mouth and destroy him by the appearance of His coming.

**7. In 1 Thessalonians 5, Paul advises believers to prepare for the Lord's coming by putting on the helmet of**

- A. faith and love
- B. the hope of salvation
- C. truth and righteousness
- D. the sword of the Spirit

**Answer: B.** Paul tells them to put on the breastplate of faith and love, and for a helmet the hope of salvation.

**8. According to 2 Peter 3, what will happen to the physical universe on the day of the Lord?**

- A. It will be restored exactly to the Garden of Eden
- B. The heavens will pass away and the elements will dissolve with fire
- C. It will be covered by a great flood of water again
- D. It will be ruled by the man of lawlessness for a season

**Answer: B.** Peter teaches that the heavens will pass away with a loud noise, and the elements will be dissolved with fire as the earth is burned up.

**9. In the Parable of the Sheep and the Goats, who do the goats on the left hand represent?**

- A. The dead in Christ
- B. The scoffers who mocked the Second Coming
- C. The wicked who failed to show practical love to the needy
- D. The false prophets who performed lying wonders

**Answer: C.** The goats represent the wicked who are condemned because they did not feed the hungry, clothe the naked, or visit the sick and imprisoned.

**10. What reason did Paul give in 1 Thessalonians 4 for writing to the believers about the Second Coming and the dead in Christ?**

- A. So that they would know the exact day and hour
- B. So that they would not grieve as others do who have no hope
- C. So that they could identify the man of lawlessness
- D. So that they would stop working and wait for the Lord

**Answer: B.** Paul wrote to them so that they would not be ignorant about those who have died, and so they would not grieve like the rest of the world who have no hope, telling them to "comfort one another with these words."
