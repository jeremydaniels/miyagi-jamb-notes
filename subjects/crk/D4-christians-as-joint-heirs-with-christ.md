# JAMB Crk Notes: Christians as Joint Heirs with Christ

## What JAMB expects

This topic is Section D, Topic 4 of the JAMB CRK syllabus: Christians as joint heirs with Christ. It has one content item:

- Gal. 3:23-29; 4:1-7

By the end of these notes, you should be able to:

1. describe how Christians are joint heirs with Christ;
2. recognize that males and females are all children of God;
3. indicate the benefits of being joint heirs with Christ.

## The teaching of Paul to the Galatians

**Content item: Gal. 3:23-29; 4:1-7**

In his letter to the Galatians, Paul addresses a major problem: some early Christians (often called Judaizers) were teaching that to be a true Christian, one must also obey the old Jewish laws (such as circumcision). Paul wrote to explain that the Law was temporary and that faith in Jesus Christ brings believers into full sonship and inheritance, replacing the old system.

### The Law as a schoolmaster (Galatians 3:23-29)

Paul explains the historical purpose of the Law using a clear comparison:

1. **Life before faith:** Before the Christian faith came, people were "kept under the law, shut up unto the faith which should afterwards be revealed." The Law acted like a prison or a strict guard.
2. **The Law as a schoolmaster:** Paul calls the Law our "schoolmaster" (the Revised Standard Version uses the word "custodian"). In the ancient Roman and Greek world, a schoolmaster (or *paidagogos*) was a strict servant whose job was to supervise a young child, discipline him, and lead him safely to his actual teacher. The Law did this by showing people their sins and pointing them toward Christ.
3. **The arrival of faith:** "But after that faith is come, we are no longer under a schoolmaster." Because Christ has come, the temporary guardian is no longer needed.
4. **Becoming children of God:** Paul declares that all who believe are the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus. 
5. **Putting on Christ:** He explains that as many as have been "baptized into Christ have put on Christ." Baptism is compared to putting on a new set of clothes—the believer is now covered by Christ's righteousness.
6. **Abraham's seed:** Because believers belong to Christ, they are considered "Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise." They inherit the spiritual blessings God promised to Abraham long before the Law was given.

### The illustration of the underage heir (Galatians 4:1-7)

To further explain this change in status, Paul uses an example from family law:

1. **The underage heir:** An heir (someone meant to inherit his father's wealth) who is still a child is treated no differently from a servant or slave. Even though he is legally the "lord of all," he cannot control his inheritance. He is under the control of "tutors and governors" until the time set by his father.
2. **Spiritual childhood:** In the same way, before Christ came, humanity was like an underage child, living in bondage under the "elements of the world" (the basic religious rituals and laws).
3. **The fulness of time:** When the perfect, appointed time arrived ("the fulness of the time"), God sent His Son. Jesus was "made of a woman" and "made under the law."
4. **Redemption and adoption:** Jesus was sent for a specific purpose: "To redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons."
5. **The proof of sonship:** Because believers are now adopted as full, mature sons, God has "sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father." 
6. **The conclusion:** Therefore, the believer is no longer a servant, but a son. And if a son, then "an heir of God through Christ."

## Objective i: describe how Christians are joint heirs with Christ

An **heir** is someone who has the legal right to receive property, a title, or a promise when the owner dies. A **joint heir** (or co-heir) is someone who shares that inheritance equally with someone else.

The Bible teaches that Jesus Christ is the natural Son of God and the heir to all things. According to Paul's teaching in Galatians, Christians become joint heirs with Christ through a specific spiritual process:

1. **Through Faith:** Christians do not become heirs by obeying the Jewish laws, but by having faith in Jesus Christ (Galatians 3:26).
2. **Through Baptism:** By being baptized into Christ, believers "put on Christ." God looks at them and sees the righteousness of Jesus, treating them as He would treat His own Son.
3. **Through Adoption:** Christians are not born naturally into God's family; they are brought in through **adoption**. In the Roman world, an adopted son was given the exact same legal rights, privileges, and inheritance as a biological son. By redeeming humanity from the curse of the Law, Christ made it possible for believers to receive the "adoption of sons" (Galatians 4:5).
4. **Through the Holy Spirit:** The proof of this inheritance is the presence of the Holy Spirit. God places the Spirit of His Son into the hearts of believers. This Spirit gives Christians the confidence to address God intimately as "Abba, Father" (Abba is an Aramaic word used by Jesus, meaning "Father" or "Daddy").
5. **Sharing the Inheritance:** Because Christians are officially recognized as God's children, they are no longer servants. As sons, they automatically become heirs. Because they are "in Christ," they share equally in everything that belongs to Christ. 

## Objective ii: recognize that males and females are all children of God

One of the most revolutionary statements in the New Testament is found in Galatians 3:28: 

> *"There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus."*

In the ancient world, society was heavily divided by strict barriers:
- **Race and Religion (Jew vs. Greek):** Jews believed they were God's chosen people and looked down on Gentiles (Greeks).
- **Social Status (Bond vs. Free):** Slaves (bondservants) were considered property and had no legal rights, while free citizens had full privileges.
- **Gender (Male vs. Female):** Women had very few legal rights. They could rarely inherit property equally with men, could not testify in certain courts, and were generally treated as inferior.

Paul teaches that **in Christ, all these human barriers are destroyed**. Because everyone is saved exactly the same way—by faith and baptism into Christ—everyone has the exact same standing before God. 

### Application to Nigerian life
In some traditional Nigerian cultures, male children are highly preferred, and only sons are allowed to inherit their father's land and property. Females are sometimes seen as temporary members of the family who will eventually marry and leave. 

The Christian teaching in Galatians challenges this cultural mindset. In the spiritual family of God, there is absolute equality. God does not value male Christians more than female Christians. Both males and females:
- Receive the exact same Holy Spirit.
- Are equally called the "children of God."
- Are joint heirs who will inherit the exact same eternal promises.
There is no gender discrimination in salvation or in God's love.

## Objective iii: indicate the benefits of being joint heirs with Christ

Being a joint heir with Christ brings massive spiritual benefits and changes the believer's status completely. Paul highlights several key benefits in these passages:

1. **Freedom from bondage (Liberty):** The believer is no longer a slave to the strict rules of the Law, nor are they under the bondage of the "elements of the world" (superstitions, basic worldly rituals, and fear). They are free.
2. **Intimate relationship with God:** Through the Holy Spirit, believers do not approach God with the terror of a slave before a master. They approach Him with the love and confidence of a child before a father, crying out, "Abba, Father."
3. **The gift of the Holy Spirit:** The Holy Spirit is placed directly into the believer's heart, acting as a guide, comforter, and the seal of their inheritance.
4. **Equality in the Church:** Because everyone is a joint heir, believers enjoy a united brotherhood and sisterhood where racism, tribalism, and gender discrimination have no place. They are "all one in Christ Jesus."
5. **Inheriting the promises of Abraham:** Long before the Law existed, God promised Abraham that through his "seed," all nations would be blessed. By belonging to Christ, Christians become Abraham's spiritual seed and inherit these promises, which include justification by faith and eternal life.

### Comparison: Life under the Law vs. Life as Joint Heirs

| Feature | Life under the Law (Before Christ) | Life as Joint Heirs (After Christ) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Status** | Servant / Underage child / Slave | Mature son / Adopted child |
| **Authority** | Ruled by a "schoolmaster" (custodian) | Guided by the Holy Spirit |
| **Relationship to God** | Distant, based on strict rules | Intimate ("Abba, Father") |
| **Social Structure** | Divided (Jew/Gentile, male/female) | United (All one in Christ) |
| **Inheritance** | Waiting for the promise | Actively possessing the promise |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Schoolmaster / Custodian** | A strict servant or guardian (*paidagogos*) in ancient times who supervised a child; Paul uses this to describe the Law. |
| **Heir** | A person legally entitled to receive the property or rank of another upon their death. |
| **Joint heir (Co-heir)** | Someone who shares an inheritance equally with another person (Christians share with Christ). |
| **Adoption** | The legal process where a person takes another into their family as their own child, granting them full rights and inheritance. |
| **Abba** | An Aramaic word meaning "Father" or "Daddy," showing deep affection and closeness. |
| **Elements of the world** | The basic religious principles, rituals, or worldly forces that kept humanity in spiritual bondage before Christ. |
| **Abraham's seed** | The descendants of Abraham; spiritually, it refers to Jesus Christ and all those who belong to Him by faith. |

## Exam tips and common mistakes

- **Do not confuse the "schoolmaster" with Jesus.** A common mistake is thinking Jesus is the schoolmaster. Paul specifically says the **Law** was our schoolmaster to bring us to Christ.
- **Know the translation differences.** If your exam uses the Revised Standard Version (RSV), it will say "custodian" instead of "schoolmaster". Both mean the exact same thing in this context.
- **Remember the pairs of equals.** Be able to list the three barriers broken down in Galatians 3:28: Jew and Greek, bond and free, male and female. JAMB sometimes asks which pair is *not* mentioned (e.g., "rich and poor" is not explicitly in this verse, though the principle applies).
- **Understand the timing.** God sent His Son "when the fulness of the time was come" (Galatians 4:4). This means God chose the perfect, exact moment in history for Jesus to be born.
- **The meaning of Abba.** Always remember that "Abba" translates directly to "Father". It represents the intimate benefit of adoption.

## Quick summary

1. Before faith in Christ, humanity was kept under the Law, which acted as a strict schoolmaster or custodian.
2. Once faith came, the schoolmaster was no longer needed, because believers are made children of God through faith.
3. By being baptized into Christ, believers "put on Christ" and become Abraham's spiritual seed.
4. In Christ, all social barriers are destroyed: there is no difference between Jew and Greek, slave and free, or male and female; all are equal children of God.
5. Paul compares humanity before Christ to an underage heir who is no different from a servant, kept under guardians.
6. In the "fulness of time," God sent His Son to redeem those under the law and give them the "adoption of sons."
7. Because believers are adopted sons, God puts the Holy Spirit in their hearts, allowing them to call Him "Abba, Father."
8. The benefits of this adoption include freedom from the Law, the gift of the Holy Spirit, equality in Christ, and being joint heirs of God's eternal promises.

## Practice questions

**1. According to Paul in Galatians, before faith came, what served as a schoolmaster to bring people to Christ?**

- A. The Prophets
- B. The Holy Spirit
- C. The Law
- D. John the Baptist

**Answer: C.** Paul states in Galatians 3:24, "Wherefore the law was our schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ, that we might be justified by faith."

**2. Which of the following barriers did Paul say is completely broken down because believers are all one in Christ?**

- A. Rich and poor
- B. Male and female
- C. Young and old
- D. Saint and sinner

**Answer: B.** Galatians 3:28 says, "There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus."

**3. In Galatians 4, Paul compares an heir who is still a child to a:**

- A. schoolmaster
- B. king
- C. servant
- D. priest

**Answer: C.** Galatians 4:1 states, "Now I say, That the heir, as long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all."

**4. How do believers practically "put on Christ" according to Galatians 3:27?**

- A. By offering sacrifices
- B. By being baptized into Christ
- C. By keeping the Jewish laws
- D. By giving to the poor

**Answer: B.** The passage says, "For as many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ."

**5. God sent forth His Son to redeem those under the law so that they might receive the:**

- A. adoption of sons
- B. forgiveness of the Romans
- C. elements of the world
- D. tablets of Moses

**Answer: A.** Galatians 4:5 says Christ came "To redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons."

**6. Because believers are adopted as sons, God sends forth the Spirit of His Son into their hearts, crying:**

- A. Holy, Holy, Holy
- B. Hallelujah
- C. Abba, Father
- D. Lord, have mercy

**Answer: C.** Galatians 4:6 states, "And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father."

**7. Christians are considered joint heirs with Christ because they are spiritually counted as the seed of:**

- A. David
- B. Moses
- C. Peter
- D. Abraham

**Answer: D.** Galatians 3:29 concludes, "And if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise."

**8. What does the Aramaic word "Abba" mean in the context of Paul's teaching?**

- A. Master
- B. Father
- C. Spirit
- D. Saviour

**Answer: B.** "Abba" is an affectionate Aramaic word meaning Father, demonstrating the close, intimate relationship believers now have with God.

**9. When did God send forth His Son, according to Galatians 4?**

- A. When the Roman Empire fell
- B. When the Law was destroyed
- C. In the fulness of the time
- D. During the days of King Herod

**Answer: C.** Galatians 4:4 explicitly states, "But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son..."

**10. Which of the following is NOT a benefit of being a joint heir with Christ according to the passage?**

- A. Becoming "lord of all" in the physical world
- B. Freedom from the bondage of the elements of the world
- C. Having the Holy Spirit in one's heart
- D. Being no longer treated as a servant by God

**Answer: A.** Becoming "lord of all" in the physical world is not a benefit taught here. The passage focuses on spiritual freedom, the Holy Spirit, sonship, and not being a servant to the law or the elements of the world.
