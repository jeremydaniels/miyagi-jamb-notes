# JAMB Irk Notes: Revelation of the Glorious Qur’ān

## What JAMB expects

This topic is Part 1, Topic 1 of the JAMB Islamic Religious Knowledge syllabus: Revelation of the Glorious Qur’ān. It covers the earliest days of Islam when the divine message was first sent to Prophet Muhammad (SAW). 

This file covers the following content items from the syllabus:

- (ii) His reaction to the first revelation and its importance

## Visits of the Prophet (SAW) to Cave Hira

**Objective i: analyse the Prophet’s (SAW) visits to Cave Hira and the purpose.**

Before Prophet Muhammad (SAW) received the first revelation, he lived in Makkah during the period of *Jāhiliyyah* (the Age of Ignorance). The Makkan society was filled with idol worship, gambling, drinking, and immoral practices. The Prophet (SAW) disliked these practices and never took part in them.

As he approached the age of forty, he developed a strong desire for solitude. He began taking provisions (food and water) and retreating to **Cave Hira** (*Ghār Hirā’*). 

### Location of the Cave
Cave Hira is a small cave located near the top of **Jabal an-Nūr** (The Mountain of Light), which is about two miles outside the city of Makkah. 

### Purpose of his visits
1. **Meditation and Reflection (*Tahannuth*):** The Prophet (SAW) went there to meditate and reflect on the creation of the heavens and the earth. He sought a deeper understanding of the universe and its Creator.
2. **Seeking the Truth:** He wanted to discover the true way to worship God, leaning towards the pure monotheism (the *Hanīf* religion) of Prophet Ibrāhīm (Abraham).
3. **Isolation from a Corrupt Society:** The cave provided a quiet, peaceful refuge away from the noise, idol worship, and moral decay of Makkan society. 

He would stay in the cave for days or weeks, especially during the month of Ramadān, returning to his wife, Khadījah (RA), only to collect more provisions before going back.

## The First Revelation and the Prophet's Reaction

**Objective ii: describe the Prophet’s reaction to the first revelation and its importance.**

In the year 610 CE, when Prophet Muhammad (SAW) was forty years old, the first revelation came to him in Cave Hira. This momentous event occurred during the last ten days of the month of Ramadān, on a night known as **Laylat al-Qadr** (The Night of Decree/Power).

### The Event
While the Prophet (SAW) was meditating, the Angel Jibrīl (Gabriel) appeared to him and gave him a command:
- The Angel said: "Read!" (*Iqra!*).
- The Prophet (SAW), who could not read or write, replied: "I am not a reader" (*Mā anā bi qāri’in*).
- The Angel caught him and squeezed him tightly until he could hardly bear it, then released him and said again, "Read!"
- The Prophet gave the same answer. This happened three times.
- Finally, the Angel recited the first five verses of **Sūrah al-‘Alaq (Q.96:1-5)**:
  > "Read: In the name of thy Lord Who createth,
  > Createth man from a clot.
  > Read: And thy Lord is the Most Bounteous,
  > Who teacheth by the pen,
  > Teacheth man that which he knew not." (Pickthall's translation)

### The Prophet's Reaction
The experience was terrifying and physically exhausting. The Prophet (SAW) reacted with intense **fear, shock, and trembling**. 
1. He immediately left the cave and hurried home to his wife Khadījah (RA), his heart beating severely.
2. He cried out to her, "Cover me! Cover me!" (*Zammilūnī! Zammilūnī!*). She quickly wrapped him in blankets until his fear subsided.
3. He told her what had happened, saying, "I fear for my life."
4. Khadījah (RA) comforted him with historic words of reassurance: "Never! By Allah, Allah will never disgrace you. You keep good relations with your family, help the poor and the destitute, serve your guests generously, and assist the deserving calamity-afflicted ones."
5. Khadījah then took him to her cousin, **Waraqah bin Nawfal**, a blind, elderly Christian scholar who knew the Torah and the Gospel.
6. Waraqah listened and confirmed his Prophethood, saying: "This is the same *Nāmūs* (the Angel of Revelation, Jibrīl) whom Allah sent to Moses." Waraqah warned him that his people would eventually drive him out of Makkah.

### Importance of the First Revelation
- **The Dawn of Islam:** It marked the official beginning of his Prophethood (*Nubuwwah*) and the start of the Islamic faith.
- **The Descent of the Qur'an:** It was the moment the Qur'an first began to be sent down to mankind, bringing divine guidance.
- **Emphasis on Knowledge:** The very first word revealed was "Read," highlighting that Islam places immense importance on literacy, education, and the pursuit of knowledge.

## Different Modes of Revelation

**Objective iii: differentiate between the modes of revelation.**

*Wahy* is the Arabic word for divine revelation. Allah did not communicate with His Prophets in only one way. The Glorious Qur'an outlines the different modes of revelation in **Sūrah ash-Shūrā (Q.42:51)**:

> "And it was not (vouchsafed) to any mortal that Allah should speak to him unless (it be) by revelation or from behind a veil, or (that) He sendeth a messenger to reveal what He will by His leave. Lo! He is Exalted, Wise." (Pickthall's translation)

Based on this ayah and authentic Hadīth, Islamic scholars classify the modes of revelation into the following categories:

| Mode of Revelation | Description | Examples / Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Inspiration (True Dreams / *Ilhām*)** | The message is put directly into the Prophet's heart, or seen in a True Dream (*Ar-Ru'yā as-Sādiqah*). | For six months before the cave, the Prophet (SAW) saw true dreams that came true the next day like the dawn. This is the simplest form of *Wahy*. |
| **2. Behind a Veil (*Min warā'i hijāb*)** | Hearing a divine voice directly without seeing the speaker. | Allah spoke directly to Prophet Mūsā (Moses) at Mount Sinai. Allah also spoke to Prophet Muhammad (SAW) from behind a veil during his night journey and ascension (*Al-Isrā' wal-Mi‘rāj*). |
| **3. Through a Messenger / Angel** | Allah sends an Angel (usually Jibrīl) to deliver the exact words to the Prophet. This was the main way the Qur'an was revealed. | **a. Like the ringing of a bell (*Salsalat al-Jaras*):** The Prophet heard a heavy, ringing sound. He said this was the hardest mode for him; he would sweat profusely even on a very cold day, and if he was on a camel, it would sit down under the spiritual weight.<br><br>**b. Angel in Human Form:** Jibrīl would sometimes appear as a man (often resembling a handsome companion named Dihyah al-Kalbī). This was the easiest mode for the Prophet.<br><br>**c. Angel in Original Form:** The Prophet (SAW) saw Jibrīl in his true angelic form with hundreds of wings, though this only happened twice. |

## Piecemeal Revelation of the Qur'an

**Objective iv: explain why the Glorious Qur’ān was revealed piecemeal.**

Unlike earlier scriptures like the Tawrāt (Torah) which Muslims believe were given to Prophets all at once, the Glorious Qur'an was revealed **piecemeal** (in parts or stages) over a period of **23 years** (13 years in Makkah and 10 years in Madīnah). This gradual process is called *Tanjīm*.

The Qur'an itself addresses this method in two important ayāt:

**1. Sūrah al-Isrā’ (Q.17:106):**
> "And (it is) a Qur'an that We have divided, that thou mayst recite it unto mankind at intervals, and We have revealed it by (successive) revelation." (Pickthall)

**2. Sūrah al-Furqān (Q.25:32):**
> "And those who disbelieve say: Why is the Qur'an not revealed unto him all at once? (It is revealed) thus that We may strengthen thy heart therewith; and We have arranged it in right order." (Pickthall)

### Reasons for Piecemeal Revelation
1. **To Strengthen the Prophet’s Heart:** The Prophet (SAW) faced intense persecution, insults, and boycotts from the Makkans. Receiving the revelation gradually provided him with constant comfort, encouragement, and a continuous connection with Allah.
2. **To Make Memorisation Easy:** Most of the early Arabs, including the Prophet, were unlettered (unable to read or write). Revealing the Qur'an a few verses at a time made it easy for the early Muslims to memorize it perfectly. Today, millions of Muslims in Nigeria and globally memorize the entire Qur'an precisely because of its rhythmic and structured revelation.
3. **To Provide Answers as Situations Arose:** Verses were often revealed in direct response to specific questions asked by Muslims or challenges raised by non-Muslims. The specific event or question that triggered a revelation is called the "Cause of Revelation" (*Asbāb an-Nuzūl*). 
4. **Gradual Implementation of Laws:** The early Muslims needed time to change their deep-rooted habits from the days of Ignorance. For example, the prohibition of alcohol was not brought down all at once. It was revealed in three gradual stages to allow people time to adjust and willingly leave the habit.
5. **To Prove its Divine Origin:** Despite being revealed over 23 years, in different places and varying circumstances, the Qur'an contains no contradictions and reads seamlessly as one unified book.

## Names and Attributes of the Glorious Qur'an

**Objective v: identify the names and attributes of Qur’ān.**

The Glorious Qur'an refers to itself by several different names and attributes. 

**Names** refer to what the book *is*, while **attributes** describe its *qualities* and the effect it has on people.

### Proper Names of the Qur'an

| Arabic Name | English Meaning | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Al-Qur’ān** | The Recitation | The most common name. It means it is a book meant to be read and recited aloud. |
| **Al-Kitāb** | The Book | Indicates that the revelation is meant to be written down and compiled into a single volume. |
| **Al-Furqān** | The Criterion | It serves as the ultimate standard to judge or distinguish between truth and falsehood, right and wrong. |
| **Adh-Dhikr** | The Reminder | It reminds humanity of Allah, their purpose in life, and the lessons of past nations. |
| **At-Tanzīl** | The Revelation | It highlights that the text was sent down from the Most High (Allah) to the Prophet. |

### Attributes (Qualities) of the Qur'an

| Arabic Attribute | English Meaning | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Al-Hudā** | The Guidance | It guides mankind out of the darkness of ignorance and into the straight path. |
| **An-Nūr** | The Light | It illuminates the mind and the soul, removing confusion. |
| **Ash-Shifā’** | The Healing | It is a cure for physical ailments (through *Ruqyah*) and spiritual diseases of the heart (like arrogance, doubt, and envy). |
| **Al-Mubīn** | The Clear | Its core message of monotheism and morality is plain, clear, and easy to understand. |
| **Al-Majīd** | The Glorious | It holds a noble, exalted, and highly respected status above all other texts. |
| **Ar-Rahmah** | The Mercy | Its laws and guidance are a blessing and a mercy to all of mankind. |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Jāhiliyyah | The pre-Islamic period of ignorance in Arabia. |
| Tahannuth | The act of solitary meditation and devotion (practiced by the Prophet at Hira). |
| Cave Hira | The cave on Jabal an-Nūr where the first revelation occurred. |
| Laylat al-Qadr | The Night of Power/Decree in Ramadān when the Qur'an began to be revealed. |
| Wahy | Divine revelation. |
| Tanjīm | The piecemeal (gradual) revelation of the Qur'an over 23 years. |
| Asbāb an-Nuzūl | The causes or reasons for the revelation of specific verses. |
| Al-Furqān | The Criterion (a name of the Qur'an meaning it separates truth from falsehood). |
| Salsalat al-Jaras | The ringing of a bell; the hardest mode of revelation for the Prophet. |
| Hanīf | The pure monotheistic religion of Prophet Ibrāhīm. |

## Exam tips and common mistakes

*   **Age of the Prophet:** Remember that Prophet Muhammad (SAW) was **40 years old** when he received the first revelation.
*   **Location:** The cave is **Hira**, located on **Jabal an-Nūr** (The Mountain of Light). Do not confuse it with Cave Thawr (used later during the Hijrah to Madīnah).
*   **The First Revelation:** The first verses revealed were the first five verses of **Sūrah al-‘Alaq** (Q.96:1-5). It was *not* Sūrah al-Fātihah (which is the first chapter in the compiled book, but not the first revealed).
*   **Modes of Revelation:** The JAMB syllabus specifically references Q.42:51 for the modes of revelation. Know the three types mentioned in it: (1) Inspiration, (2) from behind a veil, and (3) through a messenger/angel.
*   **Duration:** The Qur'an was revealed over **23 years**. Do not mistake this for the Prophet's total lifespan (63 years).
*   **Piecemeal References:** Memorise that Q.17:106 and Q.25:32 provide the Qur'anic reasons for piecemeal revelation.
*   **Names vs Attributes:** Be able to identify *Al-Furqān* and *Adh-Dhikr* as names, and *Ash-Shifā’* or *An-Nūr* as attributes. 

## Quick summary

1. Prophet Muhammad (SAW) used to retreat to Cave Hira on Jabal an-Nūr for meditation (*Tahannuth*) to escape the corrupt Makkan society and seek the truth.
2. At the age of 40, during Ramadān (Laylat al-Qadr), Angel Jibrīl brought the first revelation: the first five verses of Sūrah al-‘Alaq.
3. The Prophet was terrified and went to his wife Khadījah, who comforted him and took him to the Christian scholar Waraqah bin Nawfal. Waraqah confirmed his Prophethood.
4. According to Q.42:51, revelation (*Wahy*) comes through inspiration, from behind a veil, or through an Angel. The hardest form for the Prophet was when it sounded like a ringing bell.
5. The Qur'an was revealed piecemeal (gradually) over 23 years to strengthen the Prophet's heart, make memorisation easy, answer emerging questions, and gradually introduce laws.
6. The Qur'an has multiple names (e.g., Al-Qur'an, Al-Kitāb, Al-Furqān) and attributes (e.g., An-Nūr, Ash-Shifā', Al-Mubīn).

## Practice questions

**1. The main purpose of Prophet Muhammad’s (SAW) early visits to Cave Hira was to**
- A. hide from his enemies in Makkah.
- B. meditate and seek the truth away from idol worship.
- C. hold secret meetings with early Muslims.
- D. write down the verses of the Qur'an.

**Answer: B.** He went to the cave for *Tahannuth* (meditation) to reflect on the universe and escape the moral decay and idol worship of the *Jāhiliyyah* society.

**2. Which Sūrah contains the very first verses revealed to the Prophet (SAW)?**
- A. Sūrah al-Fātihah
- B. Sūrah al-Muddaththir
- C. Sūrah al-‘Alaq
- D. Sūrah al-Baqarah

**Answer: C.** The first revelation consisted of the first five verses of Sūrah al-‘Alaq (Q.96:1-5), beginning with the command "Iqra" (Read).

**3. When the Prophet (SAW) was terrified by the first revelation, who confirmed to him that he had been visited by the Angel of Revelation?**
- A. Abū Bakr
- B. Alī bin Abī Tālib
- C. Waraqah bin Nawfal
- D. Abū Tālib

**Answer: C.** Khadījah took the Prophet to her cousin Waraqah bin Nawfal, a Christian scholar who confirmed that the entity was the *Nāmūs* (Angel Jibrīl) sent to past Prophets.

**4. According to authentic Hadīth, which mode of revelation was the most difficult for Prophet Muhammad (SAW) to bear?**
- A. The Angel appearing in human form
- B. True dreams
- C. Inspiration directly into the heart
- D. The revelation coming like the ringing of a bell

**Answer: D.** The Prophet stated that the revelation resembling the ringing of a bell (*Salsalat al-Jaras*) was the heaviest and most difficult for him, causing him to sweat profusely.

**5. Sūrah ash-Shūrā (Q.42:51) mentions the modes of revelation. Which of the following is NOT one of the modes mentioned in that verse?**
- A. Speaking face-to-face with Allah in His physical form
- B. Inspiration
- C. From behind a veil
- D. Sending a messenger (Angel)

**Answer: A.** The verse specifies that Allah does not speak to a mortal except by inspiration, from behind a veil, or by sending a messenger. No mortal sees Allah in His physical form in this world.

**6. The Glorious Qur’ān was revealed piecemeal to Prophet Muhammad (SAW) over a period of**
- A. 13 years
- B. 23 years
- C. 33 years
- D. 40 years

**Answer: B.** The piecemeal revelation (*Tanjīm*) took exactly 23 years: 13 years in Makkah and 10 years in Madīnah.

**7. Which of the following is a Qur'anic reason for the piecemeal revelation of the Qur'an as stated in Sūrah al-Furqān (Q.25:32)?**
- A. To make it difficult for the disbelievers to steal it.
- B. To strengthen the heart of the Prophet (SAW).
- C. Because Angel Jibrīl was tired.
- D. Because the early Muslims lacked writing materials.

**Answer: B.** Q.25:32 explicitly states that it was revealed gradually "that We may strengthen thy heart therewith."

**8. Which name of the Glorious Qur’ān translates to "The Criterion" (the standard for judging right and wrong)?**
- A. Adh-Dhikr
- B. Al-Furqān
- C. Al-Kitāb
- D. At-Tanzīl

**Answer: B.** *Al-Furqān* means The Criterion, distinguishing truth from falsehood.

**9. The attribute of the Qur'an that describes it as a cure for physical and spiritual diseases is**
- A. Ash-Shifā’
- B. Al-Mubīn
- C. Al-Majīd
- D. An-Nūr

**Answer: A.** *Ash-Shifā’* translates to "The Healing," signifying that the Qur'an cures ailments of the body and diseases of the heart (like arrogance and doubt).

**10. Cave Hira, where the first revelation took place, is located on which mountain?**
- A. Mount Uhud
- B. Jabal ar-Rahmah
- C. Jabal an-Nūr
- D. Mount Safa

**Answer: C.** Cave Hira is located near the peak of Jabal an-Nūr (The Mountain of Light) outside Makkah.
