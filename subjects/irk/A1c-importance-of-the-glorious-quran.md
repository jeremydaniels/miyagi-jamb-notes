## Examine the importance of the Glorious Qur’ān

**Objective i: examine the importance of the Glorious Qur’ān.**

The Glorious Qur'an is the central religious text of Islam. Muslims believe it is the exact, uncorrupted word of Allah (Kalamullah), revealed to Prophet Muhammad (SAW) through Angel Jibril (Gabriel) over a period of 23 years. 

Its importance cannot be overstated in the life of a Muslim. It is the primary and most authoritative source of Islamic law (*Shari'ah*), serving as the ultimate manual for human existence. Unlike previous scriptures which Muslims believe were sent to specific nations and later altered (such as the Tawrat given to Musa and the Injil given to Isa), the Qur'an is the **final revelation** meant for all of mankind until the Day of Judgement.

Allah declares the Qur'an's primary purpose at its very beginning. In Surah al-Baqarah, it states: "This is the Scripture whereof there is no doubt, a guidance unto those who ward off (evil)" (Q.2:2, Pickthall's translation). 

The importance of the Qur'an lies in its comprehensive nature. It does not just teach people how to pray; it provides a complete framework for every aspect of life. It heals the mind, regulates society, ensures justice, and outlines the path to success in this world and the Hereafter.

## The Qur'an as a source of guidance

**Content item: as a source of guidance**

The JAMB syllabus requires you to understand how the Qur'an serves as a source of guidance across five specific areas of human life: spiritual, moral, economic, political, and socio-cultural matters.

### 1. Spiritual guidance

Spiritual guidance is the core message of the Qur'an. It connects human beings to their Creator.

- **Tawhid (Oneness of Allah):** The Qur'an constantly emphasizes that there is only one God, who has no partners, parents, or offspring (e.g., Surah al-Ikhlas, Q.112). It guides Muslims away from *Shirk* (polytheism or associating partners with Allah).
- **Knowledge of Allah:** It teaches Muslims about Allah's beautiful names and attributes—that He is All-Knowing, All-Seeing, the Most Merciful, and the Supreme Creator. The famous *Ayatul Kursi* (Q.2:255) provides a profound description of Allah's power and majesty.
- **Ibadah (Worship):** The Qur'an establishes the pillars of worship, such as the observance of regular prayer (*Salah*), fasting during Ramadan (*Sawm*), and performing the pilgrimage (*Hajj*).
- **Purpose of Life:** It answers the fundamental question of human existence, stating that humans and jinn were created solely to worship Allah (Q.51:56) and to serve as His vicegerents (*Khalifah*) on earth.

### 2. Moral guidance

The Qur'an sets the standard for good character (*Akhlaq*) and distinguishes clearly between right (*Ma'ruf*) and wrong (*Munkar*).

- **Virtues:** It commands Muslims to be truthful, patient (*Sabr*), humble, and forgiving. It praises those who control their anger and pardon others.
- **Prohibition of Vices:** The Qur'an strictly forbids destructive moral behaviors such as lying, backbiting, arrogance, adultery (*Zina*), and taking innocent life.
- **Intention and Sincerity:** It teaches that actions are judged by intentions and encourages doing good deeds secretly as well as openly, solely for the pleasure of Allah, rather than for showing off to people.

### 3. Economic guidance

Islam provides a unique economic system designed to ensure the fair distribution of wealth and prevent exploitation. 

- **Halal Earnings:** The Qur'an encourages humans to work hard and seek lawful (*Halal*) means of livelihood through trade, agriculture, and legitimate professions. 
- **Prohibition of Riba (Usury/Interest):** One of the most important economic rules in the Qur'an is the strict prohibition of *Riba* (interest). While trade is permitted and blessed, exploiting people through interest is forbidden (Q.2:275).
- **Zakah and Sadaqah:** To prevent wealth from circulating only among the rich, the Qur'an mandates *Zakah* (obligatory alms) as a right of the poor over the wealth of the rich. It also strongly encourages *Sadaqah* (voluntary charity).
- **Fair Trade and Contracts:** It demands honesty in business, forbidding fraud, cheating in weights and measures (Q.83:1-3), and the hoarding of goods to inflate prices. It also commands that all financial debts and contracts be written down to avoid disputes (Q.2:282).
- **Inheritance:** The Qur'an sets out specific, mathematically precise rules for sharing the property of a deceased person among their relatives, ensuring that everyone (including women, who were previously denied inheritance in pre-Islamic Arabia) gets their rightful share.

### 4. Political guidance

The Qur'an lays down the foundational principles for a just government and state leadership.

- **Shura (Consultation):** The Qur'an establishes that affairs of the state and community should be decided by mutual consultation (*Shura*) rather than dictatorship (Q.42:38).
- **Absolute Justice:** Rulers and judges are commanded to judge with absolute fairness and justice, even if it goes against themselves, their parents, or their wealthy relatives (Q.4:135).
- **Leadership as a Trust (Amanah):** Political office is not a privilege to be exploited for personal gain; it is a heavy trust (*Amanah*) from Allah. Leaders will be held accountable by Allah for how they treated their citizens.
- **Rule of Law:** In an Islamic state, no one is above the law. The ruler and the ordinary citizen are bound by the same *Shari'ah*.

### 5. Socio-cultural guidance

The Qur'an regulates how humans interact with one another, building a healthy, cohesive community (*Ummah*).

- **Family Structure:** It provides detailed guidance on marriage (encouraging it as a protective and loving bond), divorce (outlining a step-by-step process to encourage reconciliation), and the mutual rights of husbands and wives.
- **Respect for Parents:** Treating parents with utmost kindness and respect, especially in their old age, is placed second only to the worship of Allah (Q.17:23). Saying even a word of disrespect like "Uff" is forbidden.
- **Eradication of Harmful Customs:** The Qur'an actively reformed the corrupt socio-cultural practices of Pre-Islamic Arabia (*Jahiliyyah*). For example, it strictly outlawed female infanticide (burying baby girls alive out of shame), elevated the status of women, and established brotherhood across different tribes and races, declaring that the best person in the sight of Allah is the most pious, regardless of their color or tribe (Q.49:13).
- **Social Responsibility:** It commands kindness to neighbors, orphans, travelers, and the needy, fostering a community where members care for one another's welfare.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Qur'an / Koran** | The exact word of Allah revealed to Prophet Muhammad (SAW); the primary source of Islamic guidance. |
| **Shari'ah** | Islamic law derived primarily from the Qur'an and the Hadith. |
| **Tawhid** | The absolute Oneness of Allah; the core spiritual message of the Qur'an. |
| **Ibadah** | Acts of worship or submission to Allah. |
| **Riba** | Usury or interest, which is strictly prohibited in the Islamic economic system. |
| **Zakah** | Obligatory almsgiving, a required economic pillar to purify wealth and help the poor. |
| **Shura** | Mutual consultation, a key political principle for decision-making. |
| **Amanah** | Trust; treating leadership, wealth, and life as a responsibility given by Allah. |
| **Ummah** | The global community of Muslims. |
| **Jahiliyyah** | The period of ignorance in pre-Islamic Arabia, whose harmful cultures the Qur'an reformed. |

## Exam tips and common mistakes

- **Primary Source:** Always remember that the Qur'an is the *first* and *primary* source of Islamic law and guidance. The Hadith is the secondary source.
- **Differentiating Spheres:** JAMB often asks you to classify a specific teaching. 
  - *Riba* (interest) and *Zakah* fall under **Economic** guidance.
  - *Shura* (consultation) falls under **Political** guidance.
  - *Tawhid* (Oneness of God) falls under **Spiritual** guidance.
  - Marriage and respect for parents fall under **Socio-cultural** guidance.
- **Vocabulary match:** Be familiar with terms like *Shura*, *Riba*, and *Amanah*. Questions frequently use these Arabic terms alongside their English meanings.
- **Completeness:** Understand that the Qur'an is described as a "comprehensive" or "complete" guide because it leaves no aspect of human existence unaddressed.

## Quick summary

1. The Glorious Qur'an is the ultimate, uncorrupted word of Allah and the primary source of guidance for all Muslims.
2. In **spiritual matters**, it guides humans to the worship of one God (*Tawhid*) and outlines acts of worship (*Ibadah*).
3. In **moral matters**, it defines right and wrong, commanding honesty, patience, and kindness while forbidding lying, arrogance, and oppression.
4. In **economic matters**, it ensures wealth is shared fairly by commanding *Zakah*, encouraging trade, and strictly forbidding *Riba* (interest).
5. In **political matters**, it demands that leaders rule with absolute justice, consult the people (*Shura*), and treat their position as a divine trust (*Amanah*).
6. In **socio-cultural matters**, it protects the family unit, elevates the status of women, demands respect for parents, and promotes a caring community.

## Practice questions

**1. The Glorious Qur'an is considered the primary source of guidance for Muslims because it is**

- A. the first book written in the Arabic language
- B. the exact and uncorrupted word of Allah revealed for all mankind
- C. a historical record of the Arab people
- D. a collection of the sayings of Prophet Muhammad (SAW)

**Answer: B.** Muslims believe the Qur'an is the direct word of Allah (Kalamullah), making it the supreme and primary authority in Islam.

**2. Which of the following represents the political guidance of the Qur'an?**

- A. The payment of Zakah
- B. The prohibition of Riba
- C. The principle of Shura (consultation)
- D. The establishment of daily prayers

**Answer: C.** *Shura* is the Islamic political principle of mutual consultation in governance and decision-making. Zakah and Riba are economic concepts.

**3. The Qur'an's strict prohibition of Riba (interest) is a form of guidance in**

- A. spiritual matters
- B. moral matters
- C. socio-cultural matters
- D. economic matters

**Answer: D.** The ban on interest and the promotion of fair trade are foundational to the Islamic economic system.

**4. According to the Qur'an, political leadership is primarily considered**

- A. a way to accumulate personal wealth
- B. an Amanah (divine trust) and responsibility
- C. a right inherited from one's parents
- D. an opportunity to favor one's tribe

**Answer: B.** The Qur'an teaches that leadership is an *Amanah* (trust) for which the leader will be held accountable by Allah.

**5. In socio-cultural matters, which of the following pre-Islamic practices did the Qur'an heavily condemn and abolish?**

- A. Keeping of livestock
- B. Female infanticide
- C. Trading in the marketplace
- D. Traveling for business

**Answer: B.** The Qur'an eradicated the *Jahiliyyah* (pre-Islamic) practice of burying newborn girls alive due to cultural shame.

**6. The fundamental spiritual teaching of the Glorious Qur'an is**

- A. Shura
- B. Zakah
- C. Tawhid
- D. Halal

**Answer: C.** *Tawhid*, the absolute Oneness of Allah, is the central spiritual theme and the foundation of Islamic belief.

**7. The Qur'anic command to write down debts and contracts (Q.2:282) serves as a guide for**

- A. political leadership
- B. economic and business transactions
- C. performing Hajj
- D. marital divorce

**Answer: B.** Documenting debts and contracts ensures transparency and prevents disputes in economic and business matters.

**8. The Qur'an's instruction that the best person in the sight of Allah is the most pious, regardless of race or tribe, promotes**

- A. economic monopoly
- B. socio-cultural equality and brotherhood
- C. political dictatorship
- D. spiritual polytheism

**Answer: B.** This teaching breaks down tribal and racial prejudice, guiding the socio-cultural fabric of the Muslim *Ummah*.

**9. Which of the following statements best describes the scope of the Qur'an's guidance?**

- A. It deals only with life after death.
- B. It focuses strictly on acts of worship and ignores worldly affairs.
- C. It provides a comprehensive guide for all spiritual, moral, and worldly aspects of life.
- D. It serves only as a book of laws for criminal offenses.

**Answer: C.** The Qur'an is a complete code of life, offering guidance across spiritual, moral, economic, political, and socio-cultural spheres.

**10. By commanding Muslims to treat their parents with utmost kindness and forbidding them from saying words of disrespect, the Qur'an is providing**

- A. economic guidance
- B. socio-cultural and moral guidance
- C. political governance rules
- D. rules for warfare

**Answer: B.** Instructions on how to interact with family members, especially parents, form the basis of Islamic social and moral conduct.
