# JAMB UTME Islamic Religious Knowledge (IRK) Syllabus

This is a transcription of the `irk.pdf` file (pages 139–151), which contains the JAMB UTME syllabus for Islamic Studies. The wording, spelling, punctuation, and numbering are kept exactly as in the original document.

## GENERAL OBJECTIVES

The aim of the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) syllabus in Islamic Studies is 
to prepare the candidates for the Board’s examination. It is designed to test their achievement of the 
course objectives, which are to:

1. master the Qur’ān and Sunnah as foundations of Islamic and social life;
2. be familiar with Islamic heritage, culture and civilization;
3. be acquainted with the tradition of Islamic scholarship and intellectual discourse;
4. demonstrate knowledge of Islamic moral, spiritual, economic, political and social values;
5. be exposed to the fundamental principles of Islam; and 
6. be prepared to face the challenges of life as good practising Muslims.
