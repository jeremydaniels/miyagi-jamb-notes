## Author and publication

- **Author:** Elma Shaw, a Liberian writer, publisher and filmmaker. She is based in Monrovia.
- **Full title:** *Redemption Road: The Quest for Peace and Justice in Liberia*.
- **Published:** 2008, by Cotton Tree Press, a publishing house Shaw founded in 2008. This was her first novel.
- **Do not confuse it** with other books and films called *Redemption Road* (for example, a crime thriller by John Hart and a film). Only Elma Shaw's Liberian novel is meant here.

## Category of Prose

*Redemption Road* is a work of **African prose fiction** (a novel). It belongs to **post-war literature**, because it deals with the aftermath of the Liberian civil war. The characters are fictional, but the background is the real history of Liberia. One documentary film has been made about real Liberian women whose experiences inspired the story.

## Setting and Period

**Setting (Place):** The novel is set in **Monrovia**, the capital city of **Liberia**, West Africa.

**Period (Time):** The story is set in post-civil-war Liberia, during the government of President Charles Taylor, while the country is still recovering from the war. The characters live with memories of the earlier violence.

## Socio-Political Context

- **The Liberian Civil War:** Liberia suffered a long and brutal civil war, usually dated from about 1989 to 2003 (in two phases). Many people died and many were displaced. The novel is rooted in the suffering of ordinary Liberians, especially women, in this period.
- **Peace and justice after war:** The novel's subtitle, *The Quest for Peace and Justice in Liberia*, shows its central concern: how can survivors find closure, justice and peace after such a past?

## Theme

Study guides agree that the novel deals with these themes. Check how each appears in the book itself.

### 1. Redemption and forgiveness
The title points to redemption. The main character must choose between revenge on those who wronged her and the path of redemption. Some study guides say the novel suggests forgiveness as the way forward.

### 2. Trauma and the effects of war
Characters carry painful wartime memories, and these memories torment them with regret and anger. Post-war trauma is one of the most commonly listed themes.

### 3. Justice
The subtitle and the search for Commander Cobra show that justice for victims matters in the story.

### 4. Loss and grief
Study guides list loss and grief as a theme, linked to family members and the dead.

## Plot

The story follows **Bendu Lewis** (full name given in study guides as Bendu Marie Lewis), a young Liberian woman who survived the civil war. It is set in Monrovia. Bendu and other survivors of the war struggle to find closure, justice and peace after a traumatic past. Bendu is haunted by memories of the war, and she pursues a man known as **Commander Cobra**. In this pursuit she finds much more than she expected, and she has to face a painful secret from her own wartime experience.

## Exam tips and common mistakes

*   **Know the author and setting:** *Redemption Road* is by **Elma Shaw** and is set in **Monrovia, Liberia**. Do not confuse it with novels set in Nigeria or Sierra Leone, or with other books named *Redemption Road*.
*   **Know the protagonist:** The main character is **Bendu Lewis**. The name Commander Cobra is linked to her wartime past.
*   **Know the central issues:** Redemption, forgiveness, trauma and justice after war are the safest themes to discuss.
*   **Use the subtitle:** *The Quest for Peace and Justice in Liberia* helps you remember what the novel is about.
*   **Do not invent details:** If you are unsure of a plot detail, answer with what you know from the text and avoid guessing names or events.

## Quick summary

1. *Redemption Road* is a 2008 novel by the Liberian writer Elma Shaw, published by Cotton Tree Press.
2. Its full title is *Redemption Road: The Quest for Peace and Justice in Liberia*.
3. It is African prose fiction and belongs to post-war literature.
4. It is set in Monrovia, Liberia, during Charles Taylor's government, with the civil war as its background.
5. The protagonist is Bendu Lewis, a young woman tormented by memories of the war.
6. Bendu pursues Commander Cobra and has to face a painful secret from her own wartime past.
7. Key themes include redemption, forgiveness, trauma, justice and loss.

## Practice questions

**1. *Redemption Road* was written by**

- A. Pede Hollist
- B. Elma Shaw
- C. Efua Sutherland
- D. Susanne Bellefeuille

**Answer: B.** The novel was written by the Liberian author Elma Shaw.

**2. The primary setting of the novel is**

- A. Freetown, Sierra Leone
- B. Accra, Ghana
- C. Monrovia, Liberia
- D. Lagos, Nigeria

**Answer: C.** The novel is set in Monrovia, the capital of Liberia, after the civil war.

**3. The protagonist of *Redemption Road* is**

- A. Agnes
- B. Bendu Lewis
- C. Yaa Asantewaa
- D. Anansewa

**Answer: B.** Bendu Lewis is the main character whose experiences the novel follows.

**4. Which of the following is a dominant theme in the novel?**

- A. The joy of political independence
- B. Redemption and forgiveness after war
- C. The conflict between Western and traditional African religion
- D. The humour in post-colonial marriages

**Answer: B.** The title and study guides point to redemption, forgiveness and the effects of war.

**5. The name of the man whom Bendu pursues is**

- A. Chief Duluma
- B. General Mosquito
- C. Commander Cobra
- D. Major Blood

**Answer: C.** Commander Cobra is the figure from Bendu's wartime past whom she pursues.

**6. The subtitle of *Redemption Road* is**

- A. A Story of Slavery in West Africa
- B. The Quest for Peace and Justice in Liberia
- C. The Journey of a Young Soldier
- D. Love in a Time of War

**Answer: B.** The full title is *Redemption Road: The Quest for Peace and Justice in Liberia*.

**7. The historical background of the novel is best described as**

- A. A peaceful traditional village untouched by modern politics
- B. A country fighting a war for independence from colonial rule
- C. A country recovering from the Liberian civil war
- D. A futuristic society under a strict dictatorship

**Answer: C.** The novel is set in Liberia after the civil war.

**8. In terms of genre, *Redemption Road* is best classified as**

- A. African drama
- B. Non-fiction biography
- C. African prose fiction
- D. Epic poetry

**Answer: C.** It is a novel, which is a work of prose fiction.

**9. Read this short passage: "Ada kept seeing the market fire each night. Even in the quiet of her new house, the memory returned and made her tremble." The effect described here is best called**

- A. Comic relief
- B. Trauma caused by a past event
- C. Political satire
- D. A celebration of success

**Answer: B.** The character is troubled by a painful memory from the past, which is the idea of trauma.

**10. In which year was *Redemption Road* published?**

- A. 1960
- B. 1985
- C. 2008
- D. 2020

**Answer: C.** It was published in 2008 by Cotton Tree Press.
